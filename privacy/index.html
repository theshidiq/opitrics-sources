Privacy

Last updated 17 September 2026

This site collects nothing about you. That is not a policy position so much as a description of how it is built: these are static HTML files, and there is no code on them that could collect anything.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies are set.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts. There is no <script> tag on any page — you can check with view-source.
  • No accounts, logins, forms, or newsletter signups.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, because nothing is gathered.

What is unavoidable

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub's receives your IP address and browser user-agent in order to send you the page, and may log it. That is GitHub's processing, not ours — we have no access to those logs and cannot identify visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Charts and images are embedded directly in each page rather than loaded from elsewhere, so viewing a page makes no request to any other company.

If you follow a link

Quotes link to the original comments on YouTube. Opening one takes you to Google's site, where Google's privacy terms apply. Nothing is sent to them until you click.

Comments quoted on this site

The analyses quote public YouTube comments. Author names and handles are withheld from every page — the permalink is what makes a quote checkable, and naming the commenter would expose a private person to an audience they did not ask for.

If a comment of yours is quoted here and you would like it removed, email info@opitrics.com with the link and it will be taken down. No justification is needed.
