Read this correctly

What this measures, and what it does not

Commenters are not buyers.

This says which attributes generate friction in public discussion, at what volume and intensity, with every figure traceable to comments you can open.

This does not say whether the car will sell, how demand is moving, or what the market will do. People who comment skew toward enthusiasts and strong opinions, and nothing here surveys a buyer. A decision that needs demand data needs demand data.

Sentiment is machine-scored on a sample of each theme, and sarcasm detection is weak in Japanese — themes at risk are flagged on the rows above rather than quietly scored. Counts are from the fetch date and drift afterwards.
