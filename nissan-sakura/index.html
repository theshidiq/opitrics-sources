Method, and what it gets wrong

This is not a poll. People who comment are not people who buy, and they skew toward enthusiasts and toward strong opinions. This measures what the comment section thinks, which is a proxy for public perception and a poor proxy for sales.

Sentiment is scored by machine and sampled. Comments are grouped by meaning, then a multilingual model scores a sample from each group. Sarcasm detection is weak, especially in Japanese — 3 theme(s) here are flagged for sarcasm risk, and their scores should be read as uncertain rather than wrong.

Nothing was written by hand. The clusters, counts and quotes come from the pipeline. Quotes are checked against the stored corpus before publication, so a paraphrase cannot reach the page.
